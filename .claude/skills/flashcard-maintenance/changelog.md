# Rules Version changelog

Full history of the card-format rules. Moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-08-11:
it is only load-bearing when deciding whether an existing card is stale, which
is this skill's job. Nothing here changed in the move.

The **current** version and the stamp/semantics tables stay in `CLAUDE.md` —
they are needed on every card. This file is the history behind them.

## How to use it during maintenance

To decide what a MINOR-behind card is missing, read every entry between the
card's `ver` stamp and the current Rules Version; each entry states what it
added and whether older cards need rework. A card that is MAJOR behind is a
full rebuild and you can skip the intermediate entries.

Note the entries below predate the current Anki MCP workflow. Where an entry
mentions the TSV, a third column, `#` header lines or importing, that machinery
is retired — tags are now set via `add_note`'s `tags` list or `tag_management`,
and cards are written straight to the collection. The *content* requirements
each entry describes are unaffected.

## Entries

- **2.1.1** — **The reference tail now folds automatically, and Rule 25 states the grading target.** `tl-nom`/`tl-fw`/`tl-nvv`/`tl-kl`/`tl-rm`/`tl-syn`/`tl-ant` collapse behind a `<details>` at render time — this is a script in the Anki card template, not authored HTML, so it applies retroactively to every existing note regardless of stamp, with nothing to add when a card is touched. Rule 25 records why: what's visible by default (badge, `mn` box, `bl`/`vl` lines + their examples) is the whole grading target — the folded tail is depth material, never something Again/Hard/Good/Easy depends on. Also constrains authoring going forward: anything actually needed to use a word (gender, plural, valency, auxiliary) must stay in `bl`/`vl`, never in a `tl-*` class. **No rework** — the fold and the grading target both already apply to cards at every prior version; there is nothing to change on an individual card.
- **2.1.0** — **`(common meaning)` / `(uncommon meaning)` is removed, and only meanings worth using go on a card.** The label was defined as how common a meaning is *relative to the others*, which said nothing on a single-meaning card and invited listing a sense purely to have something to mark `uncommon`. Rule 2 now states the standard directly: every listed meaning is one that will actually be used, obscure/dated/niche senses stay off the card entirely, and a card with one common meaning is complete rather than thin. **Also fixed** (no behaviour change, listed here so a rebuild picks them up): the Grammar-box templates were missing their `<br>` prefixes while every other template had them, so a card built literally from them rendered the grammar box as one run-on line; Rule 18 claimed *every* card opens with a Häufigkeit/Register badge, contradicting ⚙ Grammatikkarten which open with `⚙ Grammatikkarte` instead; two stale references to the retired TSV workflow. Depth clarified: neither axis is ever a reason to skip a card, only to shorten it, and **IT overrides `Häufigkeit` as well as `Register`** — a rare IT word still gets a Vollkarte. The stylesheet and this changelog moved out of `CLAUDE.md`. **Rework: cards at 2.0.0 are MINOR behind** — strip the `(common/uncommon)` label and drop any meaning that fails the new Rule 2. They render fine until touched.

  *Semantics note: this is the first version to* remove *a card element rather than add one. The MINOR tier is the closest fit — the structure stays valid and older cards are not broken, they simply carry something no longer wanted.*
- **2.0.0** — **Card layout overhaul: three blocks, and every example is attached to something.** The meanings now live inside a `mn` box the way the grammar lives inside `gr`, so a card reads as badge → Bedeutung(en) → Grammatik → stamp. **The free-standing examples at the bottom are gone** (Rule 4): an example exists to demonstrate one specific thing, so it sits directly under that thing — under its meaning, its valency pattern, its NVV, its Feste Wendung. **NVV and Feste Wendungen now require an example** (Rule 17), because both are constructions to be produced, not words to be recognised, and a gloss shows nothing about how they sit in a sentence. To keep the card from bloating, each is capped at **one** per card. **Order inside a meaning is fixed** (Rule 1): badge → German definition → English of that definition → short English gloss → example. The badge leads because it frames how the rest is read, and the two English lines are the other way round from 1.x — the definition's translation confirms the German, then the short gloss is the takeaway. New CSS: `.mn` for the meanings box, a readable `.fq`, and a `.nightMode` block — the stylesheet must be current in Anki, otherwise the box doesn't render. **Rework policy: rebuild on touch, not in bulk.** Older cards still render correctly and are not wrong; they get the new shape when a session touches them anyway. Depth (Vollkarte vs. Kurzkarte) is unchanged from 1.6.0.
- **1.7.0** — **Tags (Rule 23).** Four axes: `Regeln::` (mirrors the stamp), `Häufigkeit::`, `Register::`, and the flags `Karte::Grammatik` / `Karte::IT`. **Applied going forward only** — new cards and rebuilds. Existing cards are not wrong and are never retro-tagged; an untagged note simply means "not touched since tags existed", the same way a missing stamp means legacy. No rework.
- **1.6.2** — **A changed front must be reported (Rule 15a).** The front is the field Anki matches on, so a silent change orphans the review history. Any rebuild that alters the front says so in the summary as `alt → neu`; backs change silently as before. No rework.
- **1.6.1** — Consolidation, no new requirements. Merged the two overlapping purpose sections into "what goes on a card" (common vs. rare) and "how much goes on a card" (spoken + IT first). Fixed contradictions: Rule 18 still said the spoken equivalent was only for `eher schriftlich`/`Amtssprache` (1.5.0 extended it to `neutral`); two badge examples were missing it; Rule 4's "1–2 examples" now says explicitly that inline pattern examples are extra; Rule 7's "every typical preposition" narrowed to the patterns actually in use. No rework.
- **1.6.0** — **Card depth follows the two priorities: spoken German and IT.** Decide `Häufigkeit`/`Register` first, then choose Vollkarte or Kurzkarte. Spoken (`gesprochen`/`neutral`) and IT vocabulary keep the full treatment; formal, bureaucratic or rare words outside IT get a short card. Existing cards are **not wrong** — a full card is never an error — so no rework; only new cards and rebuilds are affected.
- **1.5.0** — **The spoken equivalent is mandatory on every non-`gesprochen` card**, `neutral` included, per meaning, and it may be a phrase or construction (`es gibt`, `sich melden bei`) rather than a single word. `· gesprochen: genauso` is the explicit "checked, nothing more colloquial exists". **The named equivalent gets its own card automatically.** Existing cards are structurally fine but most are missing the alternative — add it when touched.
- **1.4.1** — Clarification only: the three-question test for normal card vs. Grammatikkarte, and what stays off ⚙ cards. No rework.
- **1.4.0** — **Grammatikkarten (⚙) as a card type**, and: **nothing important stays in chat** (Rule 21) — any grammar point worth mentioning in the reply must land on a card in the same session. Existing cards need no rework.
- **1.3.0** — **Per-meaning Häufigkeit/Register badge**, and **automatic companion cards for Group 3 reflexives** (created in the same session, without asking). Older cards are structurally fine; add the badges and the missing companion when touched.
- **1.2.0** — **Common-use filter extended to the Grammatik box; scope widened from B2 to German in general.** Categories are opt-in and an empty one is correct. The filter's axis is common vs. rare, not spoken vs. written. Prune obscure idioms from older cards when touched.
- **1.1.0** — Added the **Häufigkeit / Register badge**. Cards at v1.0.0 are missing it — add it when touched.
- **1.0.0** — First versioned baseline. Spoken-language goal explicit; Perfekt not narrative Präteritum; an example per meaning *and* per variant; "update" = full recreate; cognate bonus cards; specific `tl-*` classes.
- *(pre-1.0, unstamped)* — Legacy. Known issues: generic `tl` class instead of `tl-*`; meanings without examples; narrative Präteritum; missing Perfekt auxiliary and valency.
